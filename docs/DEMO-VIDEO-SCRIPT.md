# Approved ASCII Worlds demo-video script

Saved at the user's explicit request. This script showcases the existing koi/jellyfish plugin; do not imply experimental Animation Lab designs are already released or catalog-approved.

## Direction

The demo should feel like a tiny design film, not a settings tutorial. Show something beautiful immediately, prove it runs inside Hermes, then reveal how easily users can make it theirs.

Three concepts: (A) Your workspace, a little more alive: koi in a real Hermes conversation, reveal app, toggle off/on, then jellyfish and colours. (B) Day pond to midnight ocean: warm koi in light Hermes, cut to cyan/lilac jellyfish in dark Hermes, identical framing. Use actual Hermes theme controls, not a plugin appearance setting. (C) Made of characters: close crop on glyphs, pull back to reveal a fish then full Hermes. Recommended blend: A's story, C's opening and one light/dark contrast from B.

## Main storyboard (approximately 75 seconds)

### 00:00–00:06 — A creature made of text

A close crop of a koi moving through the window. Show character texture and a naturally bending tail. No wandering cursor, preceding logo animation or giant paragraph. Small caption: **A little life in your workspace.** Slowly widen; live movement continues, not a freeze-frame pretending to be live.

### 00:06–00:15 — Reveal Hermes

Full Hermes window, clean short example conversation. Hold long enough to see readable chat and swimming creatures. Small title: **ASCII Worlds / Living text art for Hermes Desktop.** Use the actual catalog build and whole-window animation. Do not showcase excluded Chat area placement or misdescribe its layer as technically behind text.

### 00:15–00:23 — One switch changes the mood

Slow cursor to title-bar Background button. Off, brief plain-window beat, on, cursor away. Caption: **One switch. A different atmosphere.** Keep the real click visible.

### 00:23–00:35 — Make the koi yours

Open Settings → Plugins → ASCII Worlds. Two intentional changes: original koi to one clearly different palette, then one creature's individual colour. One gentle zoom to controls, widen to show result. Caption: **Choose a palette—or colour each creature.** Do not cycle every palette.

### 00:35–00:47 — Enter the jellyfish

Switch habitat, return to chat, let the motion breathe. Restrained cyan/lilac on dark Hermes. Show bell contraction and trailing tentacles. Caption: **Koi ponds. Jellyfish drifts.** Second beauty moment.

### 00:47–00:56 — Demonstrate restraint

Change fuller population to sparse. Finish with a few jellyfish and plenty of empty space. Caption: **As quiet—or as colourful—as you like.** Prioritise count over showing every slider.

### 00:56–01:07 — Keep using Hermes

Return to clean conversation, click composer, type harmless short draft while creatures move. No need to send or wait for model response. Caption: **Still your workspace. Just a little more alive.** Text remains readable.

### 01:07–01:15 — Calm final frame

Hold best wide shot; small title does not cover artwork: **ASCII Worlds / Procedural text art. Runs locally. Open source. / github.com/dudicslevente/hermes-ascii-worlds**. Do not claim marketplace availability before acceptance.

## Art direction and recording checklist

- Consistent window size/framing; charcoal or off-white outer background, modest padding/corners, soft shadow. No competing animated background or spinning 3D laptop frame.
- Few creatures, not maximum population. Slow deliberate cursor; few zooms; mainly clean cuts. Minimal motion blur preserves glyph texture.
- Short consistent captions, one at a time. No unsupported zero-CPU, focus-improvement or never-breaks claims.
- Silent with captions is sufficient for muted X playback. Quiet voiceover is an alternative; no generic corporate music. Any music must be properly licensed.
- Do Not Disturb; hide private chats, session names, accounts, notifications. Deliberately prepared example conversation.
- Record and play back a short export test before main take. Capture real motion; cut navigation delays, not defects/stutters so as to misrepresent performance.
- OpenScreen is recommended. Initial target: 16:9 H.264 MP4, 1080p at 30 fps; higher recording fps does not increase plugin animation fps. Under 90 seconds for announced hackathon demo limit.
